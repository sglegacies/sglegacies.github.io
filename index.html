Technologists predict that advanced AI could accelerate drug discovery to cure most major diseases and push human lifespans to hundreds of years.

We are not there yet.

LegaciesSG is set up for ordinary Singaporeans to pay tribute and express our respect, love and gratitude towards those who have touched and positively impacted our lives during our formative years.

While life may have taken us along different trajectories, your influence will always remain anchored in our hearts, shaping the people we have become.

Thank you, from the bottom of our hearts.
